Product Table Documentation

This document contains information about our product table structure.

## Product Table Schema

The product table is designed to store information about various products in our inventory system.

### Fields:
- product_id: Unique identifier for each product
- product_name: The name of the product
- product_description: Detailed description of the product
- product_price: The selling price of the product
- product_category: The category this product belongs to
- product_stock: Current stock quantity available
- created_at: Timestamp when the product was added
- updated_at: Timestamp when the product was last modified

### Relationships:
The product table has relationships with:
- Order items table (one-to-many)
- Category table (many-to-one)
- Supplier table (many-to-one)

### Indexes:
- Primary key on product_id
- Index on product_category for faster category-based queries
- Index on product_name for search functionality

This table is essential for our e-commerce platform and inventory management system.
